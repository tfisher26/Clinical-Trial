Coli) in Participants With Non-Muscle Invasive Urothelial Carcinoma (NMIUC) of the Urinary Bladder, Who Failed Previous Bacillus Calmette-Guérin (BCG) or Intravesical Chemotherapy

Intervention: : Inactivated UPEC-FimON — Inactivated UPEC-FimON (Uro-Pathogenic E. coli) administered intravesically once weekly for 6 weeks. The dose will be escalated across sequential cohorts.
